# Domain:
Sản phẩm cho vay cho khách hàng cá nhân trên Mobile App

# Scope:
- Unsecured loan product with a maximum limit of 100 million VND
- Recommendations for limit increase
- Automated credit scoring in near real time
- Offer customized interest rates to individual customers based on credit scoring
- Target customers: salaried employees aged 22–35
- Existing customers
- Auto approve or reject on mobile app
- Suggest and calculate the maximum amount a customer can borrow based on credit scoring
- The system can automatically determine approval and maximum eligible amount without manual intervention
- Disbursement is made immediately to the customer’s payment account
- All accounting entries are integrated into Core Banking through the ESB integration layer
- Credit scoring is a separate system that analyzes customer data to assign a score

# Output
- Requirement Document include Non-function Requirement
- High level design include Technology Architect diagram, Application Architect diagram, Data Architect diagram, Integration Architect diagram, Security Compliance
